The complete, canonical list of all Season 1 episodes — from the Prologue through the finale, Déjà Rue, plus the Lost/Cast bonus episode.

Series Premiere: The Complete Cut (Prologue + Ep. 1)

A tight-knit group of college friends sets out to document the unexplained, following cryptic online posts that hint at hidden truths. Their curiosity drives them to perform a ritual—one they believe will open the door to something bigger. What starts as a calculated experiment quickly spirals into something far more dangerous.

Prologue: The First Message

John promised he was done chasing the unexplainable. But when a cryptic email from an old friend arrives, attached with recordings too strange to ignore, he finds himself drawn back into the mystery that once consumed his life.

Episode 1: Outsider

Rue Asfaw reunites with her childhood friend James, expecting a quiet visit. Instead, she finds herself surrounded by strangers, all preparing for a trip no one will fully explain. Tensions surface quickly as Rue senses something deeper beneath the surface, and James isn’t telling her everything.

Episode 2: Crossing Over

The group sets out to perform the ritual. At first conversation is light and casual, but just before everything starts, James gets serious.

Episode 3: The Space Between

The ritual worked… but no one knows what that means. Still dazed, the group tries to pull themselves together as the weight of what they’ve started begins to settle in. Rue is the most shaken.

Episode 4: The Things We Hear

The group wakes to quiet calm, trying to convince themselves things feel normal. But Rue knows better. Something changed last night. The fog gets closer, and something starts dragging itself through the trees, watching… waiting.

Episode 5: Precious Little Things

Stranded and out of gas, the group encounters a mysterious gas station and its cryptic clerk. They soon learn that getting back on the road might require more than just fuel, and the journey ahead is far more dangerous than they imagined.

Episode 6: Shadows in the Fire

A reckless act of defiance at the gas station unleashes a terrifying, unseen predator that stalks the group. The entity invades their minds, using their own voices and deepest fears as weapons, resulting in a devastating encounter that will cost the group dearly.

Episode 7: Night Special - The Weight of Knowing

The late shift at a diner is usually quiet, but for Priya, something feels off. When a mysterious woman arrives, Priya is left with a chilling realization that the world is not as solid as it seems.

Episode 8: The Path We Burn

Stranded and mourning a devastating loss, the shared trauma tears the group apart. As they try to salvage what's left, fear, grief, and anger finally boil over, pushing one of them to their breaking point.

Episode 9: Fever Pitch

Exhausted, the team pushes on through a dangerous storm, forcing them to take shelter at the Second Well Inn—a place that feels warm and welcoming, but comes with a very specific set of rules.

Episode 10: The Breaking Point

Supplies are low and Malik is fading, pushing the group’s tension to a breaking point. Rue wakes to a disorienting new reality where the rules of existence have changed, isolating her in a shifting nightmare.

Episode 11: All's Not Well

The fragile peace of the Second Well Inn shatters, plunging the group into chaos. With the group fractured and friends missing, the few who remain arm themselves and head into the woods. A trail of blood leads to a site of worship hidden in the trees—but to find the others, they must descend into the well.

Episode 12: Trina's Dream - Where the Stars Fall

The snow hasn't stopped falling, and the village is running out of patience. Trina is haunted by visions of a burning sky, a curse that marks her as a danger to neighbors desperate for someone to blame. When fear turns into violence, she and Micha are forced to run toward the forbidden woods—where the cold is absolute, and the only way out is to fall.

Episode 13: The Coldest Peace

Deep beneath the earth, the Winter Lady offers an eternal, frozen slumber—a temptation that has already claimed the rest of the group. To break the trance, Rue must reject the entity's twisted mercy and make a desperate wager that forces a chaotic escape back to the surface. But the sanctuary they find in the woods feels artificial.

Episode 14: Judgment of One

The ticket is bleeding. The ink has turned into a countdown of blood, and it's fading fast. When a second ticket washes up in the river, the group is forced to confront a terrifying reality—they aren't just following a trail anymore, they are being herded toward the rusted gates of the Penitent's Faire. Will they be able to save Rafa?

Episode 15: The Price of Admission

The group breaches the gates of the Penitent's Faire, only to find darkness waiting for them. Driven into the heart of the faire grounds, they are welcomed by an enigmatic Host—a showman whose hospitality knows no bounds, but whose stage demands a performance that could cost them everything.

Episode 16: The Show Must Go On | Act 1

The group has barely caught their breath before the Host pulls them back under the spotlight. What starts as a "game" quickly becomes something far more personal. But in the quiet that follows, anger turns to focus, and for the first time, they start to feel like they might have a shot at beating this place at its own game.

Episode 16: The Show Must Go On | Act 2

The Host isn't done yet. Back on stage, the group is swept into another game—and this time, he's playing favorites. As the questions get sharper and the stakes get personal, the group quietly plots their own move. But nothing could prepare them for what happens when the tent comes down and the night sky finally breaks open.

Episode 17 Finale: Déjà Rue

After a desperate escape, Rue finds herself right back where she started, navigating her exhausting home life. As double shifts, family drama, and forced reunions pile up, the cracks in her reality begin to show. A chilling warning echoes in the back of her mind: "don't let your friends get on that train." Yet some cycles are impossible to break.
